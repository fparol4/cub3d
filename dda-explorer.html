What these quantities mean

X crossing: crossing a vertical line of constant x. Y crossing: crossing a horizontal line of constant y. delta_x = |1 / ray_x| is how much the shared parameter t changes between consecutive vertical crossings; the Y interval follows the same rule.

The calculation is Q(t) = P + t × r. Parameter t is a common progress scale for both coordinates. For a non-unit ray, its physical travel length is t × |r|. The example camera rays with r = (1, ray_y) also make t equal to forward depth for an east-facing unit camera.

Traversal follows source/raycast.c, including Y first on exact ties and 1e30 for an inactive axis. This page uses an illustrative map and stores extra snapshots to let you step backward. Its coordinate drawing is generated directly from the numbers.
